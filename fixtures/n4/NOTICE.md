# N4 qualification corpus

The PDFs are unmodified files from Mozilla PDF.js `test/pdfs`, distributed under MPL-2.0.
Their immutable SHA-256 values and upstream location are recorded in `manifest.json`.
The two small CSV fixtures are repository-owned test data used to exercise missingness and
non-ASCII labels; they contain no personal or proprietary data.
The two `.txt` fixtures are repository-owned UTF-8 test data used to exercise plain-text
ingestion; they contain no personal or proprietary content and carry the corpus licence
declared in `manifest.json` (MPL-2.0).
