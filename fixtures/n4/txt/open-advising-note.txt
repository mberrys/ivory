Advisory notes, session 14 — open access extract
The programme coordinator describes advising as a scaffold rather than a gate.
Participants describe two different experiences of the same service.
Some describe choosing among options they had already been shown.
Others describe being routed toward a single recommended option.
The distinction matters because both groups report satisfaction.
A satisfaction measure alone would fold the two experiences together.
Coding therefore separates "choice among options" from "routed choice".
Codebook edition two replaces the earlier single code with these two codes.
The earlier annotations keep their original code and their original revision.
Correcting the coordinator's job title does not move any quotation.
