Methods note — coding and comparison
Thirty short interviews were coded with one codebook in two editions.
Two coders annotated overlapping passages; disagreements were retained.
Claims are compared only across the same frozen snapshot.
A frozen snapshot names the exact revisions it contains.
Re-freezing after an edit produces a new snapshot, never a rewritten one.
Supporting and challenging links are stored as separate attributed records.
An author who recorded a link is not an author who endorses the claim.
Where the evidence conflicted, both links were kept and the conflict was reported.
No ranking, scoring or automatic selection was applied to the retained quotes.
Analytical reports use the survey rows; presentation format is reported separately.
