# N6 researcher study kit — not yet conducted

## Readiness and facilitator setup

Recruit five qualitative researchers. Use only the fictional N6 study; tell each
participant that all interviews, survey values and attributed authors are
synthetic. Do not substitute scripted agents for participants.

The product's executable no-code loop must be available before conducting this
protocol. The present spike supplies portable research records, a rendered reader
and automated analysis; its command-line fixture generator is not a no-code UI.
Until that product surface exists, mark every participant row **not conducted**.

Prepare a fresh restored study per participant. Open it in the no-code research
surface and provide the rendered HTML dossier. Disable optional model providers.
Keep this facilitator protocol separate from the participant task sheet. Record
the application version, fixture/evidence digest, date, and any assistance.

## Participant task sheet

1. Open the synthetic advising study and inspect interviews 1 and 3.
2. Apply the supplied Agency codebook to an excerpt without writing code. Explain
   why “Enabled choice” or “Constrained choice” fits the passage.
3. Retrieve the coded passages and compare their interpretations.
4. Create a claim about advising. Attach supporting evidence and challenging
   evidence, providing a short rationale for each link.
5. Inspect the existing claim “Advising can enable choice.” Identify its author
   and the author of its challenging link. Explain whether authorship means they
   agree with the claim.
6. Open the “Before correction” snapshot and follow the interview 1 citation.
   Compare it with the current corrected interview. Explain why the old citation
   still displays the original wording.
7. Freeze your chosen claim and evidence context. Inspect the lineage, then
   export through the research interface without entering a command.
8. Explain one supporting and one challenging link in your own words, pointing
   to the exact evidence for each.

A participant passes only after completing the qualitative loop without code,
distinguishing supporting from challenging interpretation, distinguishing
provenance from endorsement, and distinguishing retained original from current
evidence. Facilitator rescue of a required step does not count as independent
completion. Accessibility accommodations should be recorded, not scored as rescue.
